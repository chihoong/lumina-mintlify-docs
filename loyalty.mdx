Lumina connects directly to the loyalty program you already manage in Square. Your earn rules, point balances, and rewards are all defined there — not in Lumina — so there's no rebuilding anything on the Kiosk side. Turn on the integration, and your existing program is live for guests immediately.

<Note>
  Your loyalty program rules — how guests earn points, what rewards are available, and how points are redeemed — are configured in your **Square account**, not in Lumina. Make any changes to the program itself inside Square Dashboard or the Square Loyalty settings.
</Note>

## What guests see at the Kiosk

With **Loyalty Program** turned on, the Kiosk adds these steps to the ordering flow:

- **Start of the order** — an **Earn Rewards** prompt: *"Enter your phone number or email to earn rewards on this order!"* The guest enters a phone number or an email address. No account or password is required, and guests can skip this and order without loyalty.
- **Guest is not enrolled** — the Kiosk shows **Enroll now**. The guest can join from the Kiosk.
- **Guest is enrolled** — the Kiosk shows their point balance and the rewards they can redeem.
- **Before checkout** — the Kiosk shows the points the current order will earn.
- **Redeeming** — **Select reward to redeem** applies a reward to the order, which then appears as a **Reward** line in the order summary. **See eligible items** lists the qualifying products with **Points required** and **Your balance**. If the guest does not have enough points, the Kiosk shows **Not enough points**.

Points are stored in Square, so a guest's balance is the same at the Kiosk as on your other Square channels.

### If the Kiosk shows "Rewards Not Available"

**Rewards Not Available** — *"This business does not have a rewards program set up."* — means the Kiosk could not find an active loyalty program in Square. Check, in order:

1. Your Square Loyalty program is active in your Square account.
2. The **Loyalty Program** toggle is on in Remy (see below).
3. The Kiosk has been restarted since you turned the toggle on.

## Turn Square Loyalty on or off on the Kiosk

Loyalty on the Kiosk is controlled by a single toggle in Remy — the same place whether you're switching it on or hiding it from guests.

If you have an active Square Loyalty program, turn it on for the Kiosk in Remy:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app menu in Remy">
    Tap the **circle icon in the upper left corner** of Remy to open the app menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Square Integration">
    Tap **Square Integration** from the menu options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Loyalty Program">
    Toggle **Loyalty Program** on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restart the Kiosk">
    Restart the Kiosk. Once it reloads, guests will see an **Earn Rewards** option next to the **View Order** button on the ordering screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Hide (disable) loyalty on the Kiosk

To take loyalty off the Kiosk, follow the same path and flip the toggle the other way: open the app menu → **Square Integration** → toggle **Loyalty Program** off → restart the Kiosk. Once it reloads, the **Earn Rewards** option no longer appears next to **View Order**, and guests will not see loyalty while ordering.

<Note>
  Turning the toggle off only hides loyalty on this Kiosk. It does **not** change or delete your program in Square — earn rules, point balances, and rewards stay intact and remain active on your other Square channels. You can turn it back on anytime.
</Note>

<Tip>
  If you previously added a **Loyalty Program** or **Rewards** collection to the menu (see [Promote loyalty on the Kiosk menu](#promote-loyalty-on-the-kiosk-menu) below), remove that collection too — otherwise the menu will still route guests to the loyalty signup flow.
</Tip>

### Require phone verification

{/* UNVERIFIED: this toggle is absent from the April 2026 repo snapshot. Confirm the exact label and where it sits in Remy, then replace the navigation path below with the confirmed one. */}

**Require phone verification** controls whether a guest has to confirm their phone number before points are applied to an order. With it on, the Kiosk texts a code to the number the guest entered and the guest types it in to finish earning. With it off, the guest enters a phone number or email and the points are applied without a code step.

To change it, open the app menu in Remy → **Square Integration** → toggle **Require phone verification** → restart the Kiosk.

## Promote loyalty on the Kiosk menu

Beyond the built-in Earn Rewards button, you can spotlight your loyalty program directly on the menu using collections. This gives guests a clear path to sign up before they even start ordering.

1. In Remy's **Menus** tab, open **Collections** and add a **Loyalty Program** and/or **Rewards** highlight.
2. Set a cover image for the collection. When a guest taps the cover image, they are taken directly into the rewards signup flow — Remy notes this behavior as: *"Tapping cover image will promote user signup."*

Enrollment runs through Square Loyalty and accepts a phone number or an email address.
