# Migrating to the new Kiosk

Updated May 20, 2026

Already running the classic Lumina Kiosk? This guide moves your iPad onto the new **Lumina Kiosk** app. Your Square connection, locations, and the menu you've already built in Remy carry over — you're just registering this device on the new app.

Do this at the Kiosk itself, with your **smartphone** (running Remy) in hand. You'll go back and forth between the iPad and Remy to finish.

## [1\. Create a kiosk profile in Remy](https://docs.onlumina.com/#1-create-a-kiosk-profile-in-remy)

If you don't already have a profile for this device, create one first. A profile bundles the menu, welcome screen, checkout settings, and security PIN the Kiosk will run.

1. In Remy, create a profile with **Create a New Profile** and give it a clear name.
2. See [Kiosk operations → Profiles](https://docs.onlumina.com/kiosk-operations/kiosk-operations#profiles) for what each profile contains.

## [2\. Open the new app on your iPad](https://docs.onlumina.com/#2-open-the-new-app-on-your-ipad)

On the iPad, open the new **Lumina Kiosk** app. On first launch it shows a registration screen with a **QR code** and a **6-character pairing code**.

## [3\. Register the Kiosk in Remy](https://docs.onlumina.com/#3-register-the-kiosk-in-remy)

1. In Remy, go to the **Devices** tab and tap **Register a Kiosk**.
2. **Scan QR Code** shown on the iPad — or enter the **6-character pairing code** instead.

## [4\. Set the location and profile](https://docs.onlumina.com/#4-set-the-location-and-profile)

Back in Remy, finish setup:

1. **Tap to select location** and choose this Kiosk's location.
2. **Tap to select profile** and choose the profile from step 1.

## [5\. Connect your Square reader](https://docs.onlumina.com/#5-connect-your-square-reader)

On the iPad, tap **Add a Reader** and follow the prompts to pair your Square reader over Bluetooth. Wait for the **Reader Connected** confirmation.

## [6\. Start the Kiosk](https://docs.onlumina.com/#6-start-the-kiosk)

Tap **Start Kiosk Mode** on the iPad. The Kiosk goes live on the new app — run a quick test order to confirm payments work.
