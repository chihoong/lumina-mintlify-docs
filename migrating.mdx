---
title: "Migrating Your Existing Lumina Kiosk to the New App"
sidebarTitle: "Migrating"
description: "Move your iPad from the classic Lumina Kiosk app to the new one in six steps — your Square connection and Remy menu carry over automatically."
---

If you're already running the classic Lumina Kiosk, this guide moves your iPad onto the new **Lumina Kiosk** app. Your Square connection, locations, and the menu you've already built in Remy all carry over — you're registering this device on the new app, not starting from scratch.

<Info>
  Complete this process at the Kiosk itself. Have your **smartphone running Remy** and your **iPad** within reach — you'll go back and forth between the two to finish registration.
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a kiosk profile in Remy">
    If you don't already have a profile for this device, create one now. A profile bundles the menu, welcome screen, checkout settings, and security PIN the Kiosk will run.

    1. In Remy, tap **Create a New Profile** and give it a clear, recognizable name.
    2. See [Kiosk Operations → Profiles](/kiosk-operations) for a full breakdown of what each profile contains.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the new app on your iPad">
    On the iPad, open the new **Lumina Kiosk** app. On first launch it shows a registration screen with a **QR code** and a **6-character pairing code** — keep this screen visible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register the Kiosk in Remy">
    1. In Remy, go to the **Devices** tab and tap **Register a Kiosk**.
    2. Tap **Scan QR Code** and point your phone at the QR code on the iPad — or type in the **6-character pairing code** manually if scanning isn't convenient.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the location and profile">
    Still in Remy, finish the setup:

    1. Tap the **Tap to select location** button and choose the location this Kiosk serves.
    2. Tap the **Tap to select profile** button and select the profile you created in step 1.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your Square reader">
    On the iPad, tap **Add a Reader** and follow the prompts to pair your Square reader over Bluetooth. Wait until you see the **Reader Connected** confirmation before moving on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the Kiosk">
    Tap **Start Kiosk Mode** on the iPad. The Kiosk goes live on the new app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  After the Kiosk starts, run a quick test order end-to-end to confirm payments are processing correctly. If you run into issues with the reader or connectivity, see the [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting) guide.
</Note>
