<Update label="2026-08-26" tags={["Lumina Kiosk", "Remy"]}>
  ## The August update

  Most of this release is work you no longer have to do. Guests join your rewards program with a phone number or an email and nothing else, kiosk registration finishes cleanly the first time, and your device list tells you the truth about what's actually running. It's already live on every kiosk — there's nothing to install.

  - **Rewards without an account.** A guest earns on an order by entering a phone number or an email address — no account, no app, no password. The kiosk asks once, at the start of the order: *"Enter your phone number or email to earn rewards on this order!"* Members see their point balance and what they can redeem; everyone else sees **Enroll now**. Your earn rules and rewards stay in Square, exactly where you manage them today. Whether guests have to confirm their phone number with a code before points are applied is its own toggle in Remy — see [Loyalty](/loyalty). If loyalty isn't switched on for your kiosk yet, that's a toggle in Remy too.
  - **Fast-Lane, for prepaid events.** Turn Fast-Lane on in Remy and every order on that kiosk checks out at $0.00 — no coupon code, and nothing for your staff to do per order. Guests browse and order exactly as they normally would. Orders still reach the kitchen, and order data is still recorded item by item, so you know how much of each item went out. See [Kiosk Operations](/kiosk-operations).
  - **Setup that finishes, and status you can trust.** Pairing confirms **App Lock** before it hands you a completed kiosk, and cancelling partway through releases the device cleanly, so nothing is left half-registered. In Remy's **Devices** list, every kiosk shows **Online** or **Offline** with its **Last active:** time — a kiosk that drops now shows as offline within seconds, instead of looking healthy while it's dark. See [Kiosk Operations](/kiosk-operations).
  - **Nothing breaks behind you.** Delete a menu, a group, a collection, or a profile, and every reference to it goes with it — nothing is left pointing at something that no longer exists. Preset options also make setting up an item considerably shorter.
  - **Quicker, everywhere.** Images load faster and the product grid scrolls more smoothly. Android tablets now run the same kiosk your iPads do, including devices on Android 16.

  Also in this release: clearer feedback at the kiosk when a scanned barcode isn't found, more reliable delivery of phone verification codes, and a long list of smaller fixes — including a crash when reordering menu items.

  <Tip>
    Everything here is already running on your kiosks. The only item that asks anything of you is loyalty — turn it on in [Remy](/loyalty), and guests see it on the next restart.
  </Tip>
</Update>
