  ### Rewards without an account

  A guest enters a phone number or an email at the start of the order — no account, no app, no password. Members see their balance and what they can redeem; everyone else sees **Enroll now**. Your earn rules stay in Square.

  <Frame>
    <img src="/images/Loyalty-Signin.png" alt="Kiosk prompt asking the guest to enter a phone number or email to earn rewards on this order" />
  </Frame>

  Whether guests confirm their number with a code is its own toggle — see [Loyalty](/loyalty).

  ### Fast-Lane, for prepaid events

  Turn Fast-Lane on in Remy and every order on that kiosk checks out at **$0.00** — no coupon code, nothing for staff to do per order. Orders still reach the kitchen, and item-by-item order data is still recorded. See [Kiosk Operations](/kiosk-operations).

  ### Setup that finishes, and status you can trust

  Pairing confirms **App Lock** before it hands you a finished kiosk, and cancelling partway releases the device cleanly. In **Devices**, every kiosk shows **Online** or **Offline** with its **Last active:** time — a kiosk that drops now shows as offline within seconds.

  <Frame>
    <img src="/images/whats-new-kiosk-status.png" alt="Remy device screen showing a disconnected kiosk, last active 33m ago, remote controls, and App lock verified" style={{ width: "45%" }} />
  </Frame>

  ### Nothing breaks behind you

  Delete a menu, group, collection, or profile and every reference to it goes with it. Preset options make setting up an item shorter.

  ### Quicker, everywhere

  Images load faster and the product grid scrolls more smoothly. Android tablets now run the same kiosk your iPads do.

  Also in this release: clearer feedback when a scanned barcode isn't found, more reliable delivery of phone verification codes, and a crash fix when reordering menu items.

  <Tip>
    The only item that asks anything of you is loyalty — turn it on in [Remy](/loyalty), and guests see it on the next restart.
  </Tip>
</Update>
