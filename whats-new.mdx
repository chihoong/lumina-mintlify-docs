<Update label="2026-06-15" description="v2.0.0" tags={["Lumina Kiosk", "Remy"]}>
  ## The next generation of Lumina Kiosk and Remy

  This is the biggest upgrade we've shipped: a redesigned ordering experience for your guests, and more control for you — wherever you are. We're rolling it out to a small first group of restaurants before everyone else. Upgrading takes minutes, and everything carries over.

  ### Bigger, bolder menus

  Your kiosk menu got a glow-up. Larger item images, and modifier photos that turn questions like "oat milk?" and "extra toppings?" into easy yeses.

  <Frame>
    <img src="/images/whats-new-menu.png" alt="Redesigned kiosk menu with larger item images and modifier photos" />
  </Frame>

  ### Loyalty front and center

  Loyalty is now part of the ordering flow, not an afterthought:

  - Prominent sign-in at the start of every order
  - Rewards promoted right in the menu
  - Multi-reward redemption in a single order
  - A live points balance guests can see as they go

  <Frame>
    <img src="/images/whats-new-loyalty.png" alt="Loyalty rewards panel with multi-reward redemption and live points balance" />
  </Frame>

  ### Categories organize, collections sell

  Group items into spotlights, specials, combos, and staff picks. Update them any time and push the changes to every kiosk instantly.

  <Frame>
    <img src="/images/whats-new-collections.png" alt="Collections and spotlights highlighting promotions on the kiosk menu" />
  </Frame>

  ### Sold out? Fixed in a tap

  Manage availability from your phone: 86 items and modifiers, troubleshoot, restart, and lock kiosks — all from Remy. Whether you run one kiosk or twenty-four, it's a tap away.

  <Frame>
    <img src="/images/whats-new-availability.png" alt="Manage Availability screen in Remy for 86ing items and modifiers from a phone" />
  </Frame>
