Lumina is a self-ordering kiosk platform built for restaurants that run on Square. It lets your guests browse your menu, customize items, and pay — all from an iPad at the counter — while you manage everything from your phone. This page introduces the platform, explains the two apps at its core, and points you toward the right place to get started.

## The two Lumina apps

Lumina is made up of two apps that work together. You use one; your guests use the other.

## How Lumina works

Lumina is designed around a simple division of responsibility: **Square owns your data, Remy owns your configuration, and the Lumina Kiosk owns the guest experience.**

**1. Connect Square**
Lumina pulls your menu items, modifiers, and prices directly from Square. Square is always the source of truth — if you update a price or add an item in Square, it reflects on your kiosk automatically. You never enter menu data twice.

**2. Build a kiosk profile in Remy**
A *profile* is a bundle of settings that controls how a single kiosk behaves. Each profile includes:
- The Square menu (or subset of it) to display
- A welcome screen with your branding
- Checkout settings such as tip prompts and receipt options
- A security PIN to lock the kiosk into guest mode

You can create multiple profiles — for example, a breakfast profile and a dinner profile — and switch between them from Remy at any time.

**3. Launch the Lumina Kiosk app on an iPad**
Open the Lumina Kiosk app, sign in, and apply the profile you created in Remy. The iPad immediately displays your branded welcome screen and is ready for guests. Payments flow through Square, so orders appear in your Square Dashboard and reports just like any other transaction.

<Tip>
  Keep your iPad plugged into power and mounted in a fixed position. The Lumina Kiosk app is designed to run continuously without sleep — make sure your iPad's Auto-Lock is turned off before you go live.
</Tip>
